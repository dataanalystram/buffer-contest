Who we are

HuhQueue is an independent, open-source app built on the Buffer API by Ram Prasad Somaraju, published as HuhQueue by HuhByte AI. It is not made, endorsed or operated by Buffer. The code that runs this site is at github.com/dataanalystram/buffer-contest.

How we use it

  • To read your Buffer organization, channels and posts, and to create posts, drafts and ideas in your Buffer account when you submit them, or when an agent you authorized does so within the rules you set.
  • To check each post against its network's rules before it goes to Buffer (preflight).
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or to train models.

Links, research and AI

When you write from a link, the server fetches that public page. When the agent researches a topic, it sends the topic (not your account details) to public news sources and searches.

To write a post, the text the task needs goes to the AI engine that runs it (listed under Subprocessors), or to your own provider if you saved a key. Pages, feeds and other outside content are marked as untrusted data in those prompts. After the agent has read outside content, sensitive actions (sending, scheduling, deleting) are held for you to confirm. This lowers the risk of a page steering the agent; no defence against prompt injection is complete, which is why posts wait for your approval by default.

The sample workspace at /demo never calls a model or Buffer: it shows canned results.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete: Account menu, Disconnect and delete my data, removes your account and every table that holds your data: tokens, sessions, agent tokens and connected apps, approvals, posts and runs, renders, brand kits, templates, rules, Library media, feeds and sources, AI settings and keys, upload and send sessions, and the activity log. Only the daily usage counters stay (see above).
  • Revoke: remove HuhQueue's access in your Buffer account settings at any time, and revoke any agent token or connected AI app in the app.
  • Stop agents: one switch pauses every agent at once.
  • Access or correct: most data is visible in the app; for anything else, contact us below. If you are in the EU or UK you also have your rights under the GDPR, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Cookies

One first-party cookie, hq_sid, keeps you signed in. Its CSRF partner token is sent in a header, not a cookie. There are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies, and the site loads no third-party scripts or fonts.

Contact

Questions, a data request or a correction: open an issue on the project's GitHub, or message @huhbyte.ai on Instagram. For a security problem, use GitHub's private Report a vulnerability instead of a public issue.
