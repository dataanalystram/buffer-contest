Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

HuhQueue is an independent, open-source app built on the Buffer API by Ram Prasad Somaraju. It is not made, endorsed or operated by Buffer. Source code: github.com/dataanalystram/buffer-contest.

What we store

  • Your Buffer connection. When you connect, Buffer gives this server an access token and a refresh token. They are encrypted at rest on our server and are never sent to your browser. We also store your Buffer account id, name, email and organization name so we can show who is connected.
  • Session. A random session id in an HttpOnly cookie. No tracking or advertising cookies.
  • Agent tokens you create (stored only as a SHA-256 hash), posts your agents submitted for approval, and an activity log of actions taken on your account.

What we do with it

Only what you ask: read your Buffer organization, channels and posts, and create posts, drafts and ideas in your Buffer account when you (or an agent you authorized, within the rules you set) submit them. We don't sell or share your data, and we don't use Buffer analytics.

Links you paste

To draft captions, the server fetches the public page you paste. If the operator has configured an AI model, the page's title, description and opening text are sent to that model provider to write the drafts; otherwise drafts are written on our server.

Deleting your data

Use Account → Disconnect & delete my data to delete your tokens, sessions, agent tokens, approvals and activity log immediately. You can also revoke HuhQueue's access from your Buffer account settings at any time.
