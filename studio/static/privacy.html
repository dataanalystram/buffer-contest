Who we are

HuhQueue is an independent, open-source app built on the Buffer API by Ram Prasad Somaraju, published as HuhQueue by HuhByte AI. It is not made, endorsed or operated by Buffer. The code that runs this site is at github.com/dataanalystram/buffer-contest.

How we use it

  • To read your Buffer organization, channels and posts, and to create posts, drafts and ideas in your Buffer account when you, or an agent you authorized within the rules you set, submit them.
  • To check each post against the rules of its network before it goes to Buffer (preflight).
  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising or to train models.

Links, research and AI

When you write from a link, the server fetches that public page. When the agent researches a topic, it sends the topic (not your account details) to public news searches: Google News, Bing News and the Hacker News search API.

Video links (One take, AI apps). When you give One take a link to a video (a YouTube, Vimeo or X post, or a video file), you first confirm: "I own this video or have permission to use it (e.g. my own content, a licence, or Creative Commons); I'm responsible for rights." An AI app you connect can't confirm this for you: its request waits in your Inbox until you tick it and approve. Only then does our video worker (an InstaBox machine, or a GitHub Actions runner as the fallback) fetch the video from that platform with the open-source tool yt-dlp, the same way Make a post fetches a story's video, or from the file's address. We never use your platform account, a password or cookies. The video is transcribed and cut on the worker; the clips land in your Library. If our operator has set up file storage (Cloudflare R2), the fetched source is kept there so the clips can be re-rendered without fetching it again, and deleted after 7 days; otherwise it stays on the worker only for the job and is deleted when the job ends. We keep a record of each confirmation (see the table above).

If the operator configured a model, or you saved your own key, the page's title, description and opening text go to that model provider to write captions. Without one, drafts are written on our server by rules.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete: Account menu, Disconnect and delete my data, removes your account, tokens, sessions, agent tokens, approvals, brand kit, rules, media and Library (including the Bin), runs, renders, AI settings, feeds, send codes, AI app call log and activity log.
  • Revoke: remove HuhQueue's access in your Buffer account settings at any time, and revoke any agent token on the Agents screen.
  • Stop agents: one switch pauses every agent at once.
  • Access or correct: most data is visible in the app; for anything else, contact us below. If you are in the EU or UK you also have the rights given by the GDPR, including complaining to your data protection authority.

Cookies

One first-party cookie, hq_sid, keeps you signed in. Its CSRF partner token is sent in a header, not a cookie. There are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies, and the site loads no third-party scripts or fonts.

Contact

Questions, a data request or a correction: open an issue on the project's GitHub, or message @huhbyte.ai on Instagram. For a security problem, use GitHub's private Report a vulnerability instead of a public issue.
