Your brand's editor. It learns from every correction.

Give it a topic, a link or your own photo or video. It finds and verifies the story, renders a carousel or Reel in your colors, logo and fonts, writes a caption per channel and checks all of it against your Style Ledger. Say “logo smaller” once and it never forgets. Then it lands in Buffer, on your calendar, after you approve.

From a topic to your Buffer calendar

Research with receipts

The agent searches the news, opens the source page, checks other outlets carry it, and pulls out the facts. Every step is on a timeline you can read.

Rendered in your brand style

Carousels, single cards and 9:16 Reels in your brand kit: colors, logo, font, handle and end card. Your own photos and clips get the same treatment.

A ledger that remembers

Every correction becomes a written rule. Each render and caption is checked against all of them, and a failed check names the exact rule. Then it goes to Buffer.

Counted the way Buffer counts

A caption that fits in a text box can still be rejected by the network. HuhQueue measures every draft with the same rules Buffer applies, so a post that leaves here is a post that sends.

Use it here, or from your own Claude or ChatGPT.

The web app runs the whole loop on its own. Prefer your assistant? Add HuhQueue as a connector and your own subscription does the thinking: research, render, revise, brand check and schedule are all tools.

  • Approval by default, Buffer drafts only, or trusted to schedule. You pick per agent.
  • A daily cap on every agent token.
  • One switch that stops every agent at once, and a log of everything they did.
Add it to Claude Code 
claude mcp add --transport http huhqueue \
  https://huhqueue-studio.onrender.com/mcp \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer hq_YOUR_TOKEN"
Plain REST works too. The endpoints the interface uses accept the same token.
