The service

HuhQueue helps you research, make, check and schedule social posts through your own Buffer account. The sample workspace at /demo uses made-up channels and never reaches Buffer.

Your account and Buffer

You sign in with Buffer's own OAuth. Your use of Buffer stays subject to Buffer's terms and your Buffer plan's limits. You can disconnect at any time.

Your content

You own what you upload and post. You confirm you have the rights to media you give HuhQueue, including videos you cut into clips with One take. You are responsible for what you approve and publish, including posts drafted by AI or by an agent you connect.

AI apps and agents

Agents you connect act with the policy you choose: approval by default, Buffer drafts only, or trusted to schedule. Each has a daily cap, and the kill switch stops all of them. Review AI-written text before you approve it.

Fair use

  • Don't use HuhQueue to send spam, break a network's rules or post content you don't have rights to.
  • Don't try to reach other users' data or the server's internal network, or get around rate limits and caps.
  • The sample workspace has daily caps so it stays free for everyone.
