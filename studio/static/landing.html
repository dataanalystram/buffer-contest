    Questions

    Does HuhQueue post without me?

    No. Posts made by HuhQueue or by an agent wait in your Inbox until you approve them. You can give a trusted agent permission to queue posts, with a daily cap and a kill switch.

    Is HuhQueue made by Buffer?

    No. HuhQueue is built on the Buffer API and is not affiliated with Buffer. You sign in with Buffer’s own sign-in and your posts go out through your Buffer account.

    Which channels does it work with?

    The channels you have connected in Buffer. Preflight checks each post against the rules of its network, such as X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest.

    Can my AI agent use HuhQueue?

    Yes. Claude, ChatGPT or your own script can use the same tools over MCP or REST. Agent posts wait for approval by default.

    What happens to my data when I disconnect?

    Disconnect and delete removes your account row, your encrypted Buffer tokens, your brand kit, rules, agent tokens and drafts from HuhQueue. Buffer tokens are encrypted on the server and never reach the browser.
